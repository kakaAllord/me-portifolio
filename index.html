01. About me


    

      

        

          I'm Allord Archard, an embedded systems and software developer. I started where
          the abstraction runs out — registers, interrupts, timing diagrams — and worked my way
          up through data pipelines, models, and the browser.
        

        

          That range is the point. I can take a product from a bare microcontroller reading a sensor,
          through a computer vision model that makes sense of what it sees, into an
          agentic AI layer that decides what to do about it — and give you a clean
          TypeScript dashboard to watch the whole thing run.
        

        

          I care about the unglamorous parts: deterministic timing, memory budgets, readable code,
          and interfaces that don't fight the person using them. If it ships and it holds up under
          load, that's the job done.
